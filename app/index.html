Platinum is about 200 hours away — the route below shows the order.

  1. 1 · Do the meditation first. In Chapter 1, the morning after Down to Earth, follow Lucia to the balcony and meditate. It may be missable.
  2. 2 · Play the story. Chapters 1–5, side quests as they appear. Nothing else in the story can be missed.
  3. 3 · Clean up with the checklist. After the credits, the 100% checklist opens — that is the long road to A Long Road.

One trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots. The Chapter 1 balcony meditation with Lucia disappears after Chapter 1. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter.

    Route planner — missable first, then the story, then the sweep

    The route orders the 55 trophies the way a platinum run actually flows. Do the missable step now, let the story carry you through the middle, and save the grind for free roam. Each stop shows what is still left in it and how long it should take.

    Roadmap to platinum — about 200 hours

    Five phases. Phases 1–3 are the story (roughly 60 hours of play); phase 4 is the clean-up (the long part); phase 5 is the 100% checklist and the platinum itself. Times are estimates from GTABase and KeenGamer; Push Square allows up to 300+ hours.

    Rules that protect the run

    • Save at every chapter start — there is no mission replay, so a manual save is your only rewind.
    • Never skip Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 — it is the only possibly missable step.
    • Turn on progress notifications in Settings (progress, collectibles, Criminal Profile) so nothing fills up silently.
    • Keep cheats off — a cheat disables trophies and saving.
    • Do not push toward the Irredeemable criminal profile — it is permanent.
    • Meditate with both characters together when you can — it counts twice toward the 18 spots.

    How to read the honesty labels

    • Confirmed — the method is reported by a guide that has verified it.
    • Partially confirmed — the place or shape of the task is known, but the exact steps are not yet verified.
    • Method unconfirmed — seven trophies whose descriptions hint at the task but whose steps no source has verified. The guide gives the hint and the reported lead, and nothing more.
