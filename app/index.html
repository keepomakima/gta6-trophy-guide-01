    How the phases fit together

    Phase 1 is the story: 12 mission and chapter trophies plus the gold for the final mission. Do the Chapter 1 meditation spot before the chapter ends — it is the only missable step in the whole list. Phase 2 clears the stranger strands while the map is still fresh in your head. Phase 3 is the long middle: activities, collectibles, robberies and exploration, where most of the silver trophies live. Phase 4 is money, garages and the 100% completion checklist that unlocks A Long Road — and with it, the platinum.
