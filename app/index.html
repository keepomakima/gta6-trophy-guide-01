    Session Planner — 12 sessions to the platinum

    Each session is one sitting: a goal, the trophies it can pop, and the warning that matters for that sitting. Sessions follow the story order, so the one missable trophy is covered in Session 1–2. Tick a session done when you finish it.

    Roadmap — four phases, ~200 hours

    The platinum is a journey, not a grind: story first, strangers second, the long middle of activities and collectibles third, and money plus 100% cleanup last. Hours are trophy work per phase and overlap — the journey is about 200 hours in total.

    Work the 100% completion checklist in the Progress menu during Phase 4 — it tells you exactly which buckets are short, instead of relying on memory. Gold the last Shooting Range challenges, then let A Long Road — and the platinum — pop.

    Sources & Method

    What is verified: every trophy name, description, grade (platinum / gold / silver / bronze) and hidden status was checked against Exophase and PSNProfiles on 20 Nov 2026. Where the two lists disagreed, the guide says so in the trophy’s source line.

    What is not verified: 7 trophies have no confirmed method yet. Their guides give the leading community reading and are flagged “Method unconfirmed” in the list, the detail panel and the planner — a guess is labelled a guess, never presented as a fact.

    Hours: per-trophy hours are estimates of trophy work. They overlap (driving, scanning and exploring count toward several trophies at once), so they must not be added up — the whole journey is about 200 hours.
