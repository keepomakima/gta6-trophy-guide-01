    Roadmap to the platinum

    Four phases, built from the official descriptions. Roughly 200 hours all in (PowerPyx: story 60–80 hours, difficulty 4/10). Nothing on the 100% checklist is missable — the only thing with a deadline is the Chapter 1 meditation.

    Missable trophies

    One trophy carries a real missable risk. Everything else can be finished in free roam after the credits — GTABase and PowerPyx agree on that. GTA 6 has no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

    Why nothing else is missable

    • Story trophies unlock by finishing chapters and missions — they cannot be skipped past.
    • Character quest lines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) stay open in free roam after the story.
    • Activities, robberies, collectibles and the whole 100% checklist are post-story cleanup.
    • Cheats disable trophies for the session (PowerPyx) and block saving, so play the platinum run cheat-free.
